# Welcome to Recharged

Recharged plays **Mario Strikers Charged** natively on your PC, from your own copy of the game. This launcher is where you set it up and start it; the pages on the left hold everything.

## Get playing

1. **Add your game.** On the **Game** page, choose the folder with your Mario Strikers Charged image (ISO, RVZ or WBFS), or drop the image or folder onto this window. At the first start the launcher also looks in the usual places by itself.
2. **Pick your controls.** On **Controls**, give each player a keyboard and mouse, a gamepad or a Wii Remote with Nunchuk.
3. **Press Play.** The **Play** page starts the game with your settings and shows when an update is available.

## Make it yours

- **Display** and **Audio**: the window, the picture and the sound levels.
- **Miis**: online play needs a Mii; create one here or import yours from Dolphin.
- **Teams** and **Matches**: the sidekicks each captain starts with, and presets of the match settings and cheats.
- **Gameplay**: optional tweaks to the original game, and HD texture packs.

## Good to know

- Changes are kept when you press **Save** at the bottom right (or `Ctrl+S`); **Revert** brings back the saved settings; the launcher asks before it closes with unsaved changes.
- Hover over a setting to read what it does. A gamepad works too: the D-pad moves, A chooses, B goes back.
- Something wrong? **About** has your build's details and the logs folder for a bug report, and the community on Discord.

You can open this guide again with the light bulb at the bottom, beside **Revert**.
