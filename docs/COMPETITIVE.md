# Competitive play (planned for 1.2)

Recharged 1.2 is about competitive play: challenge a friend in a few clicks,
or play a [start.gg](https://www.start.gg) tournament without typing in a
single score. It builds on the online play of 1.1. This is our plan: we are
working on it, but details can still change if we find better options.

The game's own online mode stays as it is: going online from the main menu
works as on the Wii, with friend codes, ranked matches and rankings.

## How a match feels

1. Open Recharged and go to **Competitive**, your lobby. It shows your open
   sets, who is online right now and your recent opponents.
2. Challenge an opponent, or let a tournament pair you with one.
3. You both press **Ready to play!**
4. The game starts by itself, **straight into the captain draft**: no intro,
   no menus, no friend codes to trade, no searching. You pick your captain and
   sidekicks in the game's own draft, just like on the Wii.
5. You play the series under the set's rules, for example best of 3, three
   minutes per game, no Mega Strikes.
6. Back in the lobby you see the result. Ask for a rematch, or move on to
   your next set.

A *set* is your series of games against one opponent. While you wait or play,
your Discord status shows it: waiting for an opponent, or in a set against Bob
with the score.

## Challenge a friend

- Type a player's Discord name and choose a rule set. They get
  **"Alice challenges you"** with Accept and Decline.
- Anyone who is signed in and has Recharged open can be challenged. The lobby
  shows who is online.
- A challenge lasts only as long as Recharged is open: close Recharged and
  your open challenges are gone, with nothing to clean up.

## Tournaments on start.gg

start.gg runs the tournament: sign-up, check-in, the bracket and the
standings. Recharged takes care of everything from "you have been paired" to
"the result is in".

- **Once:** link your start.gg account to your Recharged profile in the
  launcher.
- **On the day:** sign up and check in on start.gg as usual. When the bracket
  pairs you, the set appears in your lobby with the event's rules.
- **Play:** you both press Ready to play!, the game starts and you play the
  series.
- **The result goes in by itself.** Both games send what they saw. When they
  agree, our server enters the result and the game scores on start.gg, the
  bracket moves on, and your next set shows up in the lobby.
- **Disputes** stay with the tournament's organizers on start.gg, as always.

If you have played online tournaments on start.gg before, for example in
Rocket League, you know the routine: copy a lobby name and password, one
player hosts and sets up the match by hand, and after the series you type in
the scores and wait for your opponent to confirm them. With Recharged none of
this is needed: the meeting, the rules and the report happen automatically.

## Your Recharged profile

- You sign in with your **Discord** account. The sign-in happens in your
  browser, so Recharged never sees your password.
- Your name in Recharged is your Discord name.
- The profile is you, not your PC: sign in on any computer you play on.
- From Discord, Recharged gets only your account's ID, name and picture:
  nothing about your messages, friends or servers.

## The same rules for both players

Every set has one rule set, the same for both players: the competitive
gameplay tweaks (such as no Mega Strikes, fast terrain and Thunder Island
walls), the skill level, the game length or goal limit, the number of games
and the stadium. Tournament organizers choose the rule set of their event; for
a challenge you pick one from the server's list. start.gg only pairs the
players and keeps the scores.

Players of the USA and the European version cannot play against each other,
because the two versions of the game differ inside. Every tournament is for
one version.

## Later

After 1.2 we would like to add a Recharged ladder based on the same checked
results, and Discord messages that tell you when your set is ready or someone
challenges you.
