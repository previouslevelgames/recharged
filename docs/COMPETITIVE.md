# Competitive play (planned for 1.2)

Recharged 1.2 is about tournaments and leagues, built on the online play of
1.1. This is our plan: we are already working on it and hope to release it
soon, but details can still change if we find better options.

Retail online play stays as it is: going online in the game works as on the
Wii, with its own ranked matches and rankings.

## Your Recharged profile

- You sign in to your Recharged profile in the launcher with your **Discord**
  account. The sign-in happens in your browser, so Recharged never sees your
  Discord password.
- The profile is you, not your PC: sign in on any computer you play on. The
  game proves to the server which friend code it plays with, so your matches
  count for you.
- From Discord, Recharged gets only your account's ID, name and picture:
  nothing about your messages, friends or servers.

## Tournaments on start.gg

- Tournaments are organised on [start.gg](https://www.start.gg): sign-up,
  brackets, pairings, results and standings. You link your start.gg account to
  your Recharged profile.
- When you are paired with an opponent, you both start the game and choose
  **RANKED**: our server makes your two games find each other there.
- Recharged reports the result itself. Both games send what they saw, and once
  they agree the server enters the result on start.gg.

## Rules on our side

The server's admins set the rules of a match, the same for both players: the
competitive gameplay tweaks (such as no Mega Strikes, fast terrain and Thunder
Island walls), the skill level, the game length or goal limit, the number of
games and the stadium. start.gg only pairs the players and keeps the scores.
